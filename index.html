1. Who we are

Gilded Mythos Slots (the "game") is a social casino game for Android made by the independent developer jmdd316 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it handle, and the choices you have.

The game is entertainment only. It offers no real-money gambling and no prizes of real value. Coins and gems are virtual items for use inside the game.

2. What's stored on your device

The game saves its data in its own storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. It includes:

  • your game progress: level, coins, gems, albums, trophies, season pass and event progress;
  • your settings, such as music and sound volume, vibration and notifications;
  • purchase records the game needs to deliver what you bought exactly once, such as Google Play purchase tokens, your VIP status and piggy bank balance;
  • how many reward videos you watched today, to apply the daily limits.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or other personal information through the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional reward videos ("watch to double", "watch & win" and similar) provided by Google AdMob. Ads only play when you choose to watch one.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, including:

  • the device's advertising ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and several US states, the game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalized. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs, gem packs, offers and the premium season pass are sold through Google Play billing. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details. We never receive your card number or billing address.

When you buy something, Google Play gives the game a purchase confirmation (the product bought and a purchase token) so it can deliver your items. Purchases are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when the free wheel or your daily bonus is ready. These reminders are created on the device itself. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device settings.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the game to build profiles of you.

You never have to watch an ad to play. Ads only run when you tap a watch button.

Notifications
