Privacy Policy for Gilded Mythos Slots

Last updated: [7/25/2026]

Gilded Mythos Slots ("the App") is developed by [jmdd316]. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Username: The App asks you to enter a display name when you first play. This name is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Advertising and Analytics Data: The App uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video ads. AdMob automatically collects your device's IP address, advertising ID, app set ID, app interaction data (such as taps and video views), and diagnostic information (such as app performance data). This information is collected and processed by Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes. You can learn more about how Google handles this data at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchase Information: The App offers optional in-app purchases of virtual coins through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect, see, or store any payment card or billing information. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your email address, phone number, physical address, or any other personally identifying information beyond the display name described above.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, other than the automatic data collection performed by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing as described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

Since the App does not maintain user accounts or a backend server, your locally stored username and game data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

Changes to This Policy
